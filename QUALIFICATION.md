# Qualification and distribution status

JoptunaLearners `0.1.0` is the first public-preview line. Its executable qualification covers core
training, all fourteen model contracts, MLJ and LearnAPI behavior, checkpoints, hybrid primitives,
and optional Reactant CPU and Metal backends. Performance evidence is model-, source-, environment-,
and hardware-specific; compatibility is not presented as universal acceleration.

JoptunaLearners has no direct Joptuna dependency and installs independently. Its HPO bridge is
qualified through JoptunaIntegrations. Current hosted status belongs to this repository's public
workflow after publication. See the core
[stack overview](https://github.com/sylvainpronovost/Joptuna.jl/blob/main/docs/src/stack-overview.md).
